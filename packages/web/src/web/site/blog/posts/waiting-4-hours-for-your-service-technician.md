Are you stuck at home and bored while waiting out the dreaded 4 hours for your service technician to arrive?

**Here are the top 10 things you can do while you wait:**

What will you do with your 4 hours?

**Top 10 Things Homeowners Can Do That Are More Important Than Waiting 4 Hours for Their Service Technician**

1. **Watch Paint Dry**  
   It’s strangely therapeutic and you’ll actually see some progress—unlike checking the driveway every five minutes for a van that may never appear.
2. **Count How Many Socks Are Missing Their Partner**  
   A guaranteed way to feel productive. Bonus points if you find the mythical sock dimension portal.
3. **Read the Terms and Conditions You’ve Been Ignoring Since 2003**  
   It’s riveting stuff—there’s probably an HBO series in there somewhere.
4. **Alphabetize Your Spice Rack**  
   It’s amazing how quickly cinnamon next to cumin can change your culinary life. Plus, it’s a nice distraction from your technician’s “almost there” text.
5. **Start Learning Klingon**  
   By the time your technician arrives, you’ll at least know how to ask, “Where were you?” in a whole new language.
6. **Attempt to Fold a Fitted Sheet**  
   Four hours might just be enough time to master this dark art—or at least convince you it’s truly impossible.
7. **Try Out All the Functions of Your Fancy Coffee Machine**  
   By the technician’s arrival, you’ll be caffeinated enough to fix the issue yourself (or at least believe you can).
8. **Update Your Passwords to Something Other Than “password123”**  
   Let’s face it, it’s time—and your technician’s delay has finally forced you into doing the right thing.
9. **Recreate Your Home in Minecraft**  
   Because virtual renovations are quicker, cheaper, and don’t require awkward small talk.
10. **Write a Haiku About Waiting**  
    Four hours waiting here,  
    Watching clouds drift by slowly—  
    Service tech still late.
