## The Cost of Communication Breakdowns

In construction and field services across North America, businesses lose staggering amounts every year—not to equipment failures or material costs, but to something far more preventable: **communication breakdowns**.

Construction professionals spend a large share of their week on non-optimal activities like searching for project data, resolving conflicts, and managing miscommunication. Across the U.S. and Canadian construction sectors, those inefficiencies add up to billions of dollars a year.

## The Old Way: A Recipe for Inefficiency

Picture the reality for too many businesses today:

- Endless phone calls and voicemails
- Fragmented email chains that go nowhere
- Scattered spreadsheets with outdated information
- Customers waiting hours with no idea when help will arrive
- Office managers drowning in coordination chaos

This isn’t just frustrating—it’s expensive. Poor data and miscommunication are among the leading causes of construction rework, and rework is one of the most avoidable costs in the industry.
