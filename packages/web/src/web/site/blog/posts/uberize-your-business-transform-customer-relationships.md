Whether booking a ride or ordering food, people now expect real-time updates, transparency, and instant communication. For trade and field service businesses, this means the old way of sending a technician with no [ETA](/blog/enhancing-customer-satisfaction-through-real-time-eta-updates) or visibility no longer cuts it.

ArrivePing brings this [Uber-like](/blog/uber-effect-real-time-service) experience to the trades—improving field staff efficiency, client communication, and customer satisfaction through live updates, automated messaging, and location-based transparency.

## The Uber Effect in Field Service

Consumers are conditioned by Uber, Amazon, and DoorDash to:

- Track services in real-time
- Receive accurate ETAs
- Get proactive updates

In fact:

- 93% of customers want regular updates about their service (Salesforce Research).
- 73% of customers say valuing their time is the most important aspect of customer service (Forbes).
- Real-time transparency increases trust, which directly impacts brand loyalty and repeat business.

## How ArrivePing Improves Field Staff Efficiency

**Smarter Dispatching**  
[Automatic assignment](/dispatch-software) ranks technicians by distance, skill match, availability and current workload, so the closest qualified technician gets the job. Dispatchers can override any assignment, and automation rules decide which jobs auto-assign.

**Mobile-First Workflows**  
Technicians see job details in the app on their phone, accept jobs with one tap, and capture photos on site. Arrival is geofenced and on-site time is clocked automatically. This cuts down on paperwork and wasted admin time.

**Live Status Updates**  
Field staff mark themselves on the way with a tap, and every technician appears on a live map. Managers gain visibility, reducing scheduling errors and double-bookings.

## How ArrivePing Automates Client Communication

**Real-Time Location Sharing**  
Customers can track their technician, just like tracking an Uber driver, on a live tracking page with no app or account. Location is shared only while the technician is travelling to that appointment. Anxiety is reduced, and customers feel in control.

**Automated ETAs and Notifications**  
An [on-my-way text or email](/customer-notifications) goes out when the technician heads out, with a link to the tracking page and the ETA. If a job slips, a running-late notice goes out automatically. This keeps customers informed without anyone in the office picking up the phone.

**Branded Communication**  
Every update carries your brand, reinforcing professionalism and customer trust.
